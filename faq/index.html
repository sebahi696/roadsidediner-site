Frequently asked questions

Where is The Roadside Diner?

We have three branches around Beirut: Karout Mall (Al Hadath) · Centro Mall (Jnah) · The Town Village (Mansourieh).

What are your opening hours?

All branches are open every day: Karout Mall 11:00–22:00; Centro Mall 11:00–22:00; The Town Village 12:00–23:30.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver from all three branches across Beirut and Mount Lebanon. The app shows the delivery areas and fee for your location. You can also order for pickup.

How do I order?

The easiest way is the Roadside app. You can also call your nearest branch directly.

How can I pay?

Right now you pay cash on delivery or at pickup. Online payment is coming soon.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes – the Kids Corner has meals served with fries, a brownie and juice.

Do you have lighter options?

Yes – our Light Menu has Fit & Light burgers, salads and grilled dishes (not served at every branch).

What are Road Points?

Points you earn on every app order and exchange for rewards. The app also has the Roadside Rush game with a weekly prize.

Are prices in dollars?

Yes, menu prices are in US dollars.
